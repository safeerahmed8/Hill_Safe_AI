The Problem

J&K Mountain Roads Kill Silently

Banihal Pass, Zoji La, Jawahar Tunnel — these roads are among India's most dangerous. Sharp blind curves, fog, black ice, steep descents, and zero mobile signal create a deadly combination where accidents go undetected for hours.

When an accident happens at 2,832m altitude with no signal, the nearest hospital is 34 minutes away on average — even though every minute in a trauma situation can mean the difference between life and death.

"I witnessed accidents on these roads where help arrived too late. That's why I built HillSafe AI." — Safeer Ahmed, Developer

🚀 HillSafe AI — The Complete Solution

A real-time AI platform that monitors 100+ vehicles across 8 J&K danger zones simultaneously. The moment an accident is detected, it automatically clears the ambulance route, pre-alerts hospitals with blood type and ETA, and reduces response time from 34 minutes to 11 minutes — saving 23 minutes and potentially one life every incident.
